GLB to OBJ Converter

Convert GLB files to OBJ directly in your browser. There is no server-side conversion: your file is parsed, rebuilt in the target format and downloaded — all on your device, so even confidential models stay private and there is no file-size quota.

Convert GLB to OBJ Runs locally in your browser — your file never leaves your device.

How to convert

  1. Open the studio with the button above.
  2. Drop in your GLB file (or several files at once).
  3. Open the Convert tab, pick OBJ and click Convert & Download.
  4. The converted file downloads immediately.

Extract editable OBJ geometry from binary glTF assets. Node transforms (translation/rotation/scale/matrix) are applied during conversion, so the OBJ matches the assembled glTF scene, not the raw node-local coordinates.

Each glTF mesh becomes an OBJ object with its name preserved. UVs and normals are exported when present. .gltf files with external .bin buffers work too — select both files together.

Limitations

  • Geometry (vertices, triangles, normals) is converted. Materials, textures and animations are not part of the mesh-only pipeline and are not carried over.
  • glTF stores units in meters; models converted to glTF keep their numeric coordinates (a 20mm part becomes 20 glTF units — scale in the target tool if needed).
  • Very large models are processed in a background worker, but browser memory is the practical limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are glTF node transforms applied?

Yes — translation, rotation, scale and full matrices are baked into the exported vertices.

What about textures and materials?

Material names are carried into OBJ usemtl references, but texture data is not extracted.

Does it work with .gltf files too?

Yes — including .gltf with external .bin buffers when you select the files together, and base64-embedded buffers.
