GLTF to GLB Converter

Convert GLTF files to GLB directly in your browser. There is no server-side conversion: your file is parsed, rebuilt in the target format and downloaded — all on your device, so even confidential models stay private and there is no file-size quota.

Convert GLTF to GLB Runs locally in your browser — your file never leaves your device.

How to convert

  1. Open the studio with the button above.
  2. Drop in your GLTF file (or several files at once).
  3. Open the Convert tab, pick GLB and click Convert & Download.
  4. The converted file downloads immediately.

Packaging a multi-file .gltf (JSON + .bin + textures) into a single self-contained .glb makes assets dramatically easier to distribute, attach and load.

Select the .gltf and its .bin file(s) together in the file picker. The JSON is re-serialized with a single binary chunk; base64 data-URI buffers are repacked into binary too.

Limitations

  • Geometry (vertices, triangles, normals) is converted. Materials, textures and animations are not part of the mesh-only pipeline and are not carried over.
  • glTF stores units in meters; models converted to glTF keep their numeric coordinates (a 20mm part becomes 20 glTF units — scale in the target tool if needed).
  • Very large models are processed in a background worker, but browser memory is the practical limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert a .gltf with an external .bin?

Select both files (the .gltf and the .bin) at once when opening, then export GLB.

Are Draco-compressed files supported?

No — Draco requires its own decoder and is rejected with a clear error. Export the asset without compression first.

Is data lost in GLTF to GLB conversion?

The geometry, accessors and node structure are preserved. This tool focuses on meshes; exotic extensions may be dropped.
